About me

I’m Harriet.

I help local women in Leigh gain confidence, energy and happiness through exercise. My private studio is a space designed for women who feel stuck or anxious about the gym and want to prioritise their health.

Whether you’re new to the gym, looking to change your physique, training towards a challenge or simply wanting to feel your best, I want to help you find a way of training that you genuinely enjoy and that fits into your life.

1:1 sessions in the cabin studio, plus group training. Clients have gone on to complete HYROX, hit marathon PBs and feel stronger week to week.

The aim is simple — help you feel confident and capable, and actually enjoy training.
