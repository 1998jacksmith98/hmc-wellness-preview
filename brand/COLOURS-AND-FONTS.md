Values below are **as photographed**; indoor/window light mutes the diffuser label, so treat the merch intent (black wordmark on a light ground, or cream wordmark on black) as more reliable than the shaded label greys.

## Fonts (GUESSES only)

No type specimen, website, or brand sheet was found. These are visual guesses from the merch wordmark:

- **“HMC”**: high-contrast modern serif / didone (strong thick–thin contrast, a distinctive curved tail on the **C**). **Guesses:** Playfair Display, Bodoni, Didot, or a similar display serif. May be a custom or modified wordmark rather than a stock cut.
- **“WELLNESS”**: geometric or gothic sans, medium weight, tracked out (wide letter-spacing), set smaller under HMC. **Guesses:** Montserrat, Futura, Century Gothic, Gotham, or Avenir, with added tracking.

Do not present these font names as confirmed.
